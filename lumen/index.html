Lumen Spatial

Turns your phone and a VR headset shell into a spatial computer: camera passthrough, floating windows that stay put in your room, gaze controls, and immersive environments.

How to use it

  1. Put the phone in any Cardboard-style headset that has a camera cut-out on the front, or the passthrough will just be black.
  2. Look at something to highlight it. Hold your gaze on it, or tap the screen (headset button), to select it.
  3. Or raise a hand in front of the phone's camera: the cursor follows your hand instead of your gaze, and a pinch (thumb to index finger) clicks. Pinch and hold a window's bar to carry it. Drop your hand to go back to gaze.
  4. Look down to find the glowing Home orb. Select it to open your apps.
  5. Double-tap to recenter. To move a window, select the bar under it, look somewhere else, then hold still to drop it.
  6. Settings has controls for IPD, lens distortion, field of view and passthrough zoom so you can match your headset.
  7. Press the phone's Back gesture to leave headset mode.

Tip: in Chrome, use ⋮ › Add to Home screen to install Lumen as a fullscreen app.
